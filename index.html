About Us

More than a cup of tea

Pocket Tea started with one idea: good tea and coffee shouldn't be complicated. Every cup is made fresh, from ingredients we're genuinely proud of, by people who care how it tastes.

It's a small space built for slow mornings, quick catch-ups, and everything in between — a place you'll want to come back to, again and again.
